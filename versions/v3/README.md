# Refutation and correction of Theorem 2.10 (Conjectured), arXiv:1404.0484v1, Section 2, page 7: extensions of Patel (2026) and Raso–Kok (2026)

**Authors:** Haobo Ma, Wenlin Zhang

Extending the degree formulas of Patel (August 2026) and Raso–Kok (1 November 2026), we refute and correct Theorem 2.10 (Conjectured) of Kok, Fisher, Wilkens, Mabula and Mukungunugwa, arXiv:1404.0484v1, Section 2, page 7: the proposed addition law for maximum degrees of finite Jaco graphs fails at the distinct orders 3 and 11, even though both graphs have a unique maximum-degree vertex. Its other branch fails at orders 4 and 10. A floor formula gives the exact replacement: the addition defect is -1, 0 or 1, determined by two fractional parts rather than uniqueness. We also prove the same source's conjectured Lemma 2.2, give the partition requested on page 11, and correct the exceptional Fibonacci sums in Proposition 3.2. Patel's maximum-set identities supply the missing order condition in Theorem 2.3 and correct the edge assertion of Theorem 2.15. We give the recurrence form of Kok's 2016 Conjecture 2.16.1 and explain that its McPherson nonrepetition Conjecture 2.48.1 follows directly from the same article's Theorem 2.48 and the endpoint formula.
